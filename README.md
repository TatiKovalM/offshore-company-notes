# Offshore company notes

A one-page cheat sheet of what companies owe their registries each year in seven jurisdictions: Nevis, the Bahamas, the Marshall Islands, the British Virgin Islands, Delaware, Wyoming and Georgia.

Live page: https://tatikovalm.github.io/offshore-company-notes/

## What is in it

- annual government payments and late penalties;
- economic substance and annual report deadlines;
- the federal layer for US LLCs owned by non-residents (Form 5472, BOI after August 14, 2026);
- a short note on how the owner's own duties differ from the company's.

## Sources

Figures follow the official schedules as described in Offshora's knowledge base, for example [Delaware vs Wyoming](https://offshora.com/en/comparisons/delaware-vs-wyoming/) and the [Nevis LLC and IBC page](https://offshora.com/en/offshornye-zony/nevis.html). Fees change; check the current schedule before paying.
